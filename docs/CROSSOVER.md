# The crossover: where the GPU path overtakes the CPU

Generated by `Benchmarks/crossover.py` from the files named at the top of `Benchmarks/results/crossover_2026-09-17.csv`; nothing on this page is typed by hand. Apple M4 Max, the matrix protocol (best of up to five after a warm-up, 1.2 s budget, at least two), 10% nulls on the numeric columns.

Two questions, two tables. The first is the one a caller asks: from what row count is ArrowMetal at or ahead of the fastest CPU idiom the matrix knows (Polars eager and lazy, pyarrow and Acero on 16 batches, pandas, numpy), at every larger size measured? The second is the one the engine's router asks: from what row count is the GPU kernel faster than the single-core Swift loop the router would run instead? A row "not reached" means ArrowMetal was still behind at the largest size measured.

The bench CSV also carries `cpu-ref`, the tests' `CPUReference` oracle, which walks a closure per element and is one to two orders slower than the tight loop: it is the reason the router's CPU side is written as new loops rather than reused from the oracle.
